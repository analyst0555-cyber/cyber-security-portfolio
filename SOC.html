Security Operations Center (SOC)

A Security Operations Center monitors security events, investigates suspicious activity and coordinates responses to security incidents.

My learning and professional experience are focused on security monitoring, incident handling, investigation and operational security.

SOC Analyst Workflow

1. Monitor

Monitor security systems, alerts, events and operational activity.

2. Triage

Review alerts and determine their priority and potential impact.

3. Investigate

Collect relevant information and investigate suspicious activity.

4. Escalate

Escalate incidents according to established procedures and severity.

5. Respond

Coordinate appropriate response actions and follow security procedures.

6. Document

Record incidents, actions, findings and relevant information.
